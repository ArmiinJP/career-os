# DECISIONS

## 2026-08-21 — Roadmap is capability-first
Decision: Do not make the roadmap a tool checklist. Organize around fundamentals, engineering, distributed systems, architecture, production, AI, and career evidence.

## 2026-08-21 — No long readiness gate
Decision: User should not wait years before job applications. Market feedback is part of learning.

## 2026-08-21 — Adaptive baseline
Decision: Baseline stops when more questions have low information value. New topics get small just-in-time assessments.

## 2026-08-21 — Source hierarchy
Decision: Official/primary -> canonical books/academic -> practitioner -> community -> AI.

## 2026-08-21 — AI is replaceable
Decision: The authoritative career state must live in portable files, not in one AI provider or chat history.

## 2026-08-21 — Knowledge retention is first-class
Decision: Store concepts, insights, incidents, decisions and experiments; use retrieval and spaced review.

## 2026-08-21 — Architecture simplicity
Decision: Every additional component needs a requirement-based justification. Watch for over-architecting.

## 2026-08-21 — Month 1 focus
Decision: Distributed Data Systems + Architecture, supported by knowledge system and career evidence.
